# App Store ASO pass, 2026-10-28

## Goal and existing decisions

The goal is qualified VO2 max installs and trial starts. RevenueCat recorded 196 new customers and 12 trials from 2026-07-28 through 2026-10-27. The requested markets recorded 39 new US customers ($48), 17 German, 10 Dutch, 9 UK, 8 Australian, 8 French, 7 Taiwanese ($15), 7 Russian, and 5 Japanese.

The US title and subtitle remain `VO2 Max & Cardio Fitness` and `Fitness Age & Cardio Trends`. Apple returned the app at #11 for `vo2 max` and #47 for `cardio trends`. Keeping those exact phrases protects the current anchor and the only other confirmed top-50 US phrase. The keyword field replaces `healthkit` and `applewatch` with `vo2max`, `heart`, and `rate`. Apple returned successful US lists for the two removed queries without this app, and autocomplete surfaced the unspaced `vo2max` query and heart-rate apps. The new query ranks remain unmeasured.

The July 19 title and July 24 subtitle decisions remain binding. The title retains the VO2 Max anchor. The subtitle avoids Apple product names after App Review cited guideline 5.2.5. English promotional text remains as revised on October 13 to explain the free tier. The locale history is in commits `cccd27b`, `195ca0d`, and `36e1329`.

## Research and limits

Apple autocomplete checks covered about 999 requests, below the 1,500-call ceiling. Saved results include a 513-query letter expansion across US, DE, NL, GB, AU, FR, TW, RU, and JP, plus seed checks, 74 direct local-language checks, 47 supplemental seeds, and two additional US queries. These merge to 633 storefront/query pairs, with 221 positive inputs. A bare term is recorded as a query signal. App names returned under a prefix are recorded as app-seeded signals. No autocomplete request returned 429.

The iTunes Search API was queried for 193 terms. It returned 25 usable response lists, then returned HTTP 403 or 429 for 168 requests. A cooled-off retry of `vo2 max tracker` also returned 403. Apple’s legacy store search endpoint returned a page shell with no ordered app list. Rank values below come only from usable API lists. Failed requests are marked unavailable, not as absent from search. This prevented the required live rank checks for terms in the other eight target storefronts and some keyword removals.

Apple’s [App Store localization reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations/) confirms the US extra keyword localizations used in this pass: es-MX, ru, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ar, fr-FR, pt-BR, vi, and ko. The UK, Canada, and Australia use their own English fields, and Canada also indexes fr-CA. Apple’s [platform metadata reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/) documents the name, subtitle, promotional text, description, and keyword field limits. The keyword validator uses Apple’s 100-byte limit. Apple describes its public [Search API](https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api).
