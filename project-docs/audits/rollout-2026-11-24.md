## Good moments chosen

1. A new personal best: the app's headline number just went up past every earlier estimate.
2. Entering the user's target range from outside it: the goal the app tracks was just reached.

VO2 max moves slowly, so both are rare. That lowers ask volume on purpose: an ask on a day with no good result is the 6 per 1,000 pattern. A third moment (trend turning to improving) was left out because the trend rule would need its own product decision.

Gates unchanged: no ask before the 2nd cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when the call is made, opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, feedback never does, never before setup finishes. Never over a sheet: the 3s wait is followed by a re-check that Today is selected, the scene is active, and no What's New, recap, Settings, paywall or range sheet is up (any sheet reports through `blocksReviewPrompt`). There is no pre-prompt, no sentiment routing, and no button triggers it. Settings keeps its Rate row on `?action=write-review`. The app has no analytics capture call, so there is no `rating_asked` event. AGENTS.md's Ratings line names the new moments.

## Frequency follow-up (Rollout R)

Rollout S left only two moments, a new personal best and entering the target range. Both are rare (VO2 max moves slowly), so a typical user in their 2nd or 3rd session never reached either. Old launch and return-visit triggers had reached most users by the 3rd launch, so removing them cut ask volume.

Added a third moment, the frequent one: a new Apple Health estimate arrives (HealthKit only writes one after a qualifying outdoor workout) that is dated within the last 3 days and held or rose against the previous estimate (`ReviewMoment.isFreshEstimateThatHolds`, combined with the two rare moments in `ReviewMoment.isGoodMoment`).

| Moment | How often a typical user hits it |
|---|---|
| New estimate that held or rose (new) | Every outdoor Watch workout that produces an estimate, typically several times a month for an active user, so usually by the 2nd or 3rd session. A drop never asks. A user with no workouts that produce estimates reaches none of the three. |
| New personal best | A few times a year. Extra only. |
| Entering the target range | Once or twice a year. Extra only. |

Also: a good result recorded by a background HealthKit wake stays pending in that process, and the ask is now retried when the scene returns to active (`onChange(of: scenePhase)`), still only when a pending good result exists. Without it, a wake that finished before the user opened the app only asked if `MainTabView` appeared afterwards.

Gates unchanged: no ask before the 2nd cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when the call is made, opening the write-review page ends passive asks, feedback never does, never before setup, never over a sheet (3s wait, then re-check Today is selected, the scene is active and no sheet is up). A pending result still expires after 3 days and is memory only. No pre-prompt, no button trigger. AGENTS.md Ratings line updated.

Tests added: `FreshEstimateMomentTests` (rising and held ask, drop does not, estimate older than 3 days does not, combined moment for each kind and for slips and lower readings).
