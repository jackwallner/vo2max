B1

mediumconfirmedonboarding
Impact

The most common failed first run (access declined) lands on a screen that says the app is working and to go for a run. The button does nothing visible. The only route out is the unexplained 'Open Apple Health' link, or finding the Settings footer. A customer with years of Watch data decides the app does not work.

How it happens

B3

Custom date range keeps the clock time of the picked dates, so readings on the first and last day are dropped

mediumconfirmededge
Impact

A paid feature ('every screen follows the range you pick') silently omits readings from the first and last chosen day while the label still says 'Mar 1 to Mar 31'. Averages, change, counts and the cardio load total are wrong for the exact training block the customer chose.

How it happens
  1. Subscribe to VO2+. Open Trends, tap Custom.
  2. The sheet opens with start and end seeded from stored Date values that carry a time of day (for a fresh install, the launch time).
  3. Pick Start = Mar 1 and End = Mar 31 with the date-only pickers and tap Apply.
  4. Trends, resting heart rate, recovery and cardio load all read the window start..end with those inherited clock times.
  5. An estimate logged Mar 31 after the inherited clock time (say 6 PM when the seed time was 9 AM), or on Mar 1 before it, is outside the window.

B6

lowconfirmeddata
Impact

For the same custom range, two screens disagree on which readings are inside it. A reading on the first day can appear in the Trends list and chart but not in the Low, High, Average and count figures of the VO2+ cards, and not in the vs-previous comparison.

How it happens
  1. VO2+ subscriber opens Trends and picks Custom.
  2. Pick a Start date and an End date where the start clock time is earlier than the end clock time (for example the draft carries 10:00 into Start and 17:00 into End, or the stored end was capped to the apply time).

  4. Open the Trends or VO2+ signal cards (resting heart rate, recovery, cardio load). They call summarize(days: range.days, now: range.end), which starts at end minus the floored day count.
  5. A reading made on the first day between the start clock time and the end clock time is counted on Trends but missing from the signal cards and their summary.

Suggested fix

Give the analysis functions the range start (or pass the CardioRange) instead of rebuilding it from days, or snap custom start to startOfDay and end to end of day when the range is applied.

Evidence
let span = calendar.dateComponents([.day], from: orderedStart, to: orderedEnd).day ?? 0
return CardioRange(selection: .custom, start: orderedStart, end: orderedEnd, days: max(span, 1))
