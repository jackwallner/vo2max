Keep your cardio fitness in view.

VO2 Max reads estimates shared by compatible fitness apps and wearables through Health Connect. See your latest number, follow its trend, set a personal target and explore a broad fitness age estimate.

FREE FEATURES
• Latest VO2 max estimate and source date
• Basic cardio fitness trend and reading history
• Fitness age estimate
• Personal target range and light, dark or system appearance

VO2+ ADDS
• Resting heart rate and cardio load context
• Deep comparisons and custom date ranges
• Target outlook, age references and personal bests
• Monthly recap, optional alerts and shareable PDF reports

A DATA SOURCE IS REQUIRED
Health Connect does not calculate VO2 max. A compatible app or wearable must share VO2 max estimates first, and what you see depends on your source and permissions. Widgets and Wear OS are not included in this version.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Health readings stay on your device and the app asks for read-only access. Revoke permissions in Health Connect at any time. No ads, no account.

VO2+ is an optional upgrade: monthly, yearly or lifetime. Local prices and any eligible trial are shown before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Google Play.

The app itself is in English.

For fitness awareness only. Estimates are not medical measurements, and VO2 Max does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Discuss health concerns with a qualified clinician.
