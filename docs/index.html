How it's built and why it matters.

The Pitch

Apple Watch quietly estimates your VO2 max on outdoor walks, runs, and hikes, and then buries it in the Health app behind a chart most people never open.

This app pulls that estimate to the front: the current value, whether it's improving, a target range for your age, a year of history, and a fitness-age read. No logging, no workouts to start, no account. Open it and the number is there.

What Mattered

Nothing to log. The app has no input fields for fitness data. Everything it shows comes from HealthKit, which means it's correct or it's empty, never guessed.

Honest about estimates. VO2 max from a wrist is an approximation, and fitness age is an approximation of an approximation. The app says so and shows its method.

Empty state as a feature. Plenty of users have no estimate yet. Instead of a blank chart, the app explains exactly which workouts generate one.

Read-only by design. Requesting write access to Health would have been easier for caching. It wasn't worth the trust cost.

VO2 max and fitness age are broad estimates for fitness awareness. This app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any health condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
