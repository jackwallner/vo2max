VO2 Max Android Privacy Policy

## VO2 Max Android Privacy Policy

VO2 Max reads VO2 max estimates, optional resting heart rate, and exercise sessions from Health Connect with your permission. Health data and display caches stay on your device. We do not send health data to RevenueCat or any server. You can revoke access in Health Connect. Removing app storage deletes the cache. Reports leave your device only when you choose to share them. RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase information, anonymous app identifiers, and app version information for billing. We send RevenueCat counts, dates, and short labels about purchase screens, never health data or free text. There are no ads or accounts. Contact jackwallner+vo2@gmail.com for privacy questions.

## Delete your data

Revoke permissions in Health Connect and clear app storage or uninstall to remove the local cache. Your original Health Connect records are preserved. To request deletion of purchase, anonymous identifier or purchase-screen interaction data processed for this app, email jackwallner+vo2@gmail.com.

VO2 Max Daily Tracker is for fitness awareness. Health Connect estimates and broad reference context are not medical measurements. This app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Discuss health concerns with a qualified clinician.
