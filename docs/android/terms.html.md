## VO2 Max Android Terms

VO2 Max Daily Tracker is for fitness awareness. Health Connect estimates and broad reference context are not medical measurements. This app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Discuss health concerns with a qualified clinician.

VO2+ unlocks premium fitness context. Google Play shows the current price, billing period, and any eligible trial before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Google Play. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase. Google Play handles payments, cancellations, and refund requests.

The app reads data supplied by compatible fitness sources through Health Connect. Availability and accuracy depend on those sources. No result is a prediction, guarantee, or medical assessment. No account is required.
