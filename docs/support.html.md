# VO2 Max Support

> Help and contact for the VO2 Max and Cardio Fitness app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/vo2max/support.html

## VO2 Max Support

For support, email
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com).

## Apple Health estimates

VO2 max estimates come from Apple Health. If no estimate appears,
confirm Cardio Fitness access and record a qualifying outdoor walk, run,
or hike with Apple Watch. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Health
> VO2 Max to review access.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the VO2+ screen after reinstalling the
app or changing devices. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings >
Apple Account > Subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refunds through
[Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

If an active purchase is not recognized after restoring, include the app
version and device model in an email to
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com). Do not
send health records or other sensitive health information.
